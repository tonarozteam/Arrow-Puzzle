This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Puzzle handles information when you use the game. It applies to the current version of the game and its related services.

In short: Arrow Puzzle is designed to provide gameplay without requiring you to create an account or submit personal information directly to us.

01 Information We Collect

Arrow Puzzle does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play the game.

Gameplay-related information such as progress, settings, unlocked content, coins, or preferences may be stored locally on your device so that the game can provide its features. This information is not treated as a user account.

02 Information Collected Automatically

The game itself is not intended to collect personal information directly. If a future version integrates advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, cloud services, or another third-party SDK that collects information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe that service and the relevant data practices before or when the feature is introduced.

03 Third-Party Services

Arrow Puzzle may rely on platform services provided by the operating system or app store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play. Those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms. You should review the applicable platform's privacy policy for details about information they process.

04 Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us through the project's GitHub repository so that we can review the situation.

05 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the game's data or uninstalling the game, subject to the operating system's behavior and any platform backup features.

Because Arrow Puzzle does not require a user account, we do not maintain a personal account database for players based on the information described in this policy.

07 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

08 Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Arrow Puzzle's privacy practices, please use the contact mechanism available through the project repository.
