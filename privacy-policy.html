Arrow Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tonaroz ("Tonaroz," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Arrow Puzzle (the "Game").

We respect the privacy of all our players, including children. Arrow Puzzle is designed to provide a simple and enjoyable puzzle experience without requiring players to create an account or provide personal information to play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Puzzle does not require players to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, home address, phone number, or email address in order to play the Game.

We do not intentionally ask children to provide personal information.

Depending on the features and third-party services included in the version of the Game you use, certain technical information may be processed automatically to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Game.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the Game.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Arrow Puzzle may store certain game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Unlocked game content
  • Other information necessary for the Game to function properly

This information is used to provide and maintain your gameplay experience.

Locally stored game data may be deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, depending on your device and operating system.

3. Children's Privacy

Arrow Puzzle may be played by children, and we take children's privacy seriously.

The Game does not require children to create an account or provide personal information in order to play.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children, including:

  • Phone number
  • Email address

We do not knowingly use the Game to create personal profiles of children for advertising or other purposes.
